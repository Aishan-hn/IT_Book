# Stakeholder Map

**Use when:** you take on a new role, start a major initiative, or your quarterly review is being avoided by someone who matters.
**Time:** 45 minutes for the first draft; 15 minutes to refresh each quarter.
**Output:** a named list of stakeholders with their fears, their measures, your influence, and your next scheduled contact.

---

## Instructions

1. **List every stakeholder who can affect your outcomes** — not just the ones who report to you. Include peers, vendors, regulators, and the people who can say no.
2. **Fill the "fears" column honestly.** Stated priorities are public; fears are what actually drive behaviour. If you do not know the fear, write "unknown" and go and find out.
3. **Score influence and interest 1–5.** Influence is the ability to change your outcome. Interest is how much they care right now.
4. **Plot them** on the grid below.
5. **Set a cadence** you can sustain. A quarterly review you cancel is worse than none.
6. **Write the one sentence** you could say to each person that shows you understand them. Test it in the next conversation.

## Influence / interest grid

```
        High influence
              │
   KEEP       │      MANAGE CLOSELY
   SATISFIED  │      (co-create, frequent contact,
   (regular   │       agree decision rights)
   updates,   │
   no surprises)│
   ────────────┼────────────────────────  High interest
   MONITOR    │      KEEP INFORMED
   (light     │      (newsletters, demos,
   touch)     │       feedback loops)
              │
        Low influence
```

| Quadrant | Posture | Typical cadence |
|---|---|---|
| High influence, high interest | Manage closely; co-create decisions | Weekly or fortnightly |
| High influence, low interest | Keep satisfied; no surprises; short, sharp updates | Monthly |
| Low influence, high interest | Keep informed; use as allies and sensors | Monthly or as needed |
| Low influence, low interest | Monitor | Quarterly |

## Relationship plan

For each stakeholder with influence 4–5, complete:

```
Name: ______________________________
What success looks like for them this year: ______________________
What I need from them: ____________________________________________
What they need from me: ___________________________________________
The last time I surprised them: ___________________________________
My next three interactions:
  1. Date ______  Purpose ______________________  Format __________
  2. Date ______  Purpose ______________________  Format __________
  3. Date ______  Purpose ______________________  Format __________
What I will stop doing that irritates them: _______________________
```

---

## Quality checks

- [ ] At least three stakeholders do not report to me and are not in IT.
- [ ] Every "fear" column is filled, or explicitly marked "unknown" with a date to find out.
- [ ] Every stakeholder with influence 4–5 has a scheduled next contact.
- [ ] I have at least one stakeholder in the "keep satisfied" quadrant who is not the CEO.
- [ ] I can name the person most likely to block my strategy, and I have a plan for them.
- [ ] No stakeholder appears twice under different titles.
- [ ] I have reviewed this map with someone who knows the politics better than I do.

---

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Consequence | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Mapping only the people you like | Blind spots; late opposition | Start from decision rights, not relationships |
| Confusing interest with support | You assume an ally who is only curious | Ask what they would lose if your plan succeeds |
| Too many stakeholders | No time for any of them | Cap the "manage closely" list at seven |
| No cadence | Map becomes wallpaper | Calendar the next contact before you finish |
| Never updating | Map describes last year's organization | Refresh quarterly, and after any reorganization |
